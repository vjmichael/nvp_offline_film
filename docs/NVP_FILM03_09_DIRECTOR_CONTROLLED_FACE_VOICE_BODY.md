# FILM03-09 — Director-controlled actor voice, articulation, face and body

**Default artistic decision:** Unless the director chooses otherwise, the facial expression should match the emotional state of the performed voice. The original director-authored vocal direction supplies a provisional facial suggestion until real acoustic analysis exists. Explicit facial keyframes always override this default, including a smiling face with sad voice or an angry face with happy delivery. Body acting remains independently editable.

**Four channels:** (1) Voice: human actor, director guide, converted speech or generative dialogue; (2) Articulation: exact phonemes and actual selected PCM sample timing driving lip/jaw/tongue visemes; (3) Facial expression: independent gaze, eyebrows, lips/cheeks and emotion subject to creative director overrides; (4) Body acting: separate gestures, poses, gait and interaction. Nonhuman object ActorIDs may use no mouth or stylized reactions.

**FILM03-09A source implementation:** backend/nvp_film03_independent_facial_direction.py binds the original ActorID, VoiceID, script, frozen Take, existing 48kHz PCM and filmmaker-selected emotional direction. It stores typed viseme *candidates* plus face/body keyframes at exact 147,000 Film Time ticks per audio sample. A voice performer has a creative contributor ID/role, not legal permissions. A creator direction publishes non-destructive revisioned performance source; no rig motion or real phoneme model has been accepted yet.

Only the actual native model execution can prove phoneme measurement, and only the reviewed Blender/OpenUSD mouth rig and visual/audible tests can prove good lip sync.
