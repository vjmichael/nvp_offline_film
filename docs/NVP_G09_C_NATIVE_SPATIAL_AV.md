## Filmmaker workflow

**Edit Film → Play Film With Sound** is now a source-based A/V monitor. It requests the current exact G07 edit, bounded batches of true evaluated OpenUSD picture frames (up to 12 JPEG pictures / 0.5 seconds) and original-source 48kHz stereo PCM audio (up to one second per request). The web audio device's playback clock is the visual playhead master, instead of an independent picture timer. It prebuffers, seeks to exact original sample positions, pauses and stops safely on image/audio underrun or changed project source revisions, releases audio nodes and aborts pending requests when leaving the workspace. The older static scene inspector remains clearly identified as an inspection tool rather than continuous playback.

**Edit Film → select Take → Shape the scene's sound** offers source-specific room presets (as filmed, interior, hall, outdoors, forest, city, cavern, space), reflections, echo/reverb level, size, geometry obstruction, sound distance/air attenuation, camera-relative stereo width, optional **explicitly derived** wind/rain/snow sound events and director notes. Draft revisions are editable and restorable. Only a human-approved acoustic version reaches G07 native assembly and G08 movie export. All original G06 microphone audio remains immutable.

**Film Scenes → Record original sound** still records through the existing OS microphone adapter. A G09-C capture-health panel now shows recorded-source sample lengths and separates completed, cancelled, ongoing and interrupted sessions. It explains that hardware/audio-to-camera synchronization is not yet independently proven.

## Native/source implementations

- `backend/nvp_g09c_acoustics.py`: versioned `g09c_acoustic_heads` and immutable `g09c_acoustic_versions` in ordinary or existing G03 sealed movie SQLite. Exact G06 Take SHA/revision, source-bound review and restore. No external effects binary or unreviewed impulse-response load.
- `backend/nvp_g09c_native_audio.py`: source-time `SpatialFilmMixer`, authentic PCM-only input, real moving emitters evaluated through the frozen G06 OpenUSD xform at exact Film Time, G07 selected 3D camera listener, 10ms deterministic absolute time cells, equal-power stereo with near-ear sample delay, distance attenuation, approximate obstacle interference (including approved G09-A generated architecture), high-frequency muffling and physically motivated delayed early-reflection/reverb taps. The taps are causal and continuous across independently requested PCM chunks; the output uses a bounded soft limiter that is transparent below its high-level knee. Original source PCM is never rewritten. Uses 48kHz original sound/sample clock and G07 source edit transitions/offsets/mutes, with optional derived environmental sound from genuine G06 wind/rain/snow event directions.
- `backend/nvp_g09c_playback.py`: bounded source-evaluated 24fps picture / sample-addressable audio windows; edit, environment, actor and room sound SHA receipts. No flattened film index or fake preview-source substitution.
- `backend/nvp_g09c_routes.py` and `backend/nvp_api.py`: same director-session, film lock, encrypted project guard as G07. Fixed read/approve/mutate and bounded frame/audio playback operations. Audio return is a disposable 48kHz WAV envelope with exact source sample range headers and private no-store policy; no raw Take data or mutable source API exposed.
- `backend/nvp_g07_edit.py`: approved `acoustic_looks_sha256` in internal G07 native assembly, separate from the edit, environment and actor SHA values.
- `backend/nvp_g08_native_avi.py`: actual exported PCM audio renders from the SAME source-time acoustic mixer whenever at least one approved acoustic look is selected. The ordinary G08 mixer remains the backwards-compatible source interpretation for unapproved/unstyled films. 720-frame / 30-second native MJPEG+PCM first profile remains unchanged.
- `backend/nvp_g08_export.py`, `tools/nvp_g08_export_worker.py` and `backend/nvp_g08_sealed_export.py`: exact acoustic SHA pinned in durable ordinary/encrypted native export job records, fixed OS-custodied worker arguments and movie-render receipts. Source room-sound changes invalidate an already-selected ordinary export instead of being silently accepted. Protected output remains encrypted, not a plaintext worker scratch file.
- `backend/nvp_g09c_capture.py`: durable `g09c_capture_sessions` with native session ID, ActorID, original PCM SHA/count, OS microphone callback observations and interruption state. At native capture start, the intent is recorded; at stop, an original PCM integrity/timing receipt is committed **in the same film SQLite transaction as the native G06 original sound**. On restart/read, missing host sessions are reconciled to **interrupted**, never promoted into original audio. No CPU-audio waveform is substituted for microphone data.
- `backend/nvp_g06_native_recorder.py`, `backend/nvp_g06_filming.py` and `backend/nvp_g06_routes.py`: record OS callback frame counts, peak, host monotonic start/stop and callback gap diagnostics, then atomically seal the source and its receipt. **The original 48kHz device sample count is authoritative; host monotonic timestamps are uncalibrated observations.** Existing lock/revoke cancels the microphone and preserves fail-closed interrupted-session evidence. No user-supplied audio upload.
- `frontend/src/DirectorAVMonitor.jsx`, `director-av-monitor.css`, `DirectorSoundStage.jsx`, `director-sound-stage.css`, `DirectorFilmEditor.jsx`, `DirectorFilmingStudio.jsx`: director-first playback, spatial sound decisions, sample-clock recording status, responsive controls, explicit human approval.

## Explicit limitations, honest acceptance and next actions

1. **Hardware sync is NOT verified.** Audio sample counts and host monotonic callback timing are recorded; camera hardware timestamps, audio capture latency, resampler/device drift, clock calibration, sample drops/underruns and zero-drift physical playback need native Windows/Linux device tests. G06 original mic starts as a separately scheduled source; the director's current G07 clip placement is still the authored voice timing.
2. **Stereo, not production multichannel HRTF.** Spatial processing uses stereo panning/ITD, simple moving emitters, sphere-style geometry obstruction, near-ear delay and two reflection/diffusion taps. It is not full wavefield room acoustics, measured convolution IR, binaural HRTF, speaker-array spatial mastering, calibrated occlusion or film-mix certification.
3. **Playback is bounded and source-based.** Picture comes from native OpenUSD on the backend but WebAudio is the desktop preview sink. Frame prefetch can miss deadlines on slow renderer/hardware, causing a deliberate stop rather than silent A/V drift. No unconditional sustained 24fps device acceptance has been run. The existing physical Windows sound/video device and actual Radeon worker still require acceptance.
4. **Derived weather is not a recorded source.** Its sound is NVP-authored deterministic synthetic sound design, present only if a filmmaker explicitly enables and approves it. Original environmental microphone captures are untouched.
5. **Protected jobs:** ordinary G08 movie export retains independently supervised source/AVI verification. Protected-film G08/G09 derivations remain in-memory and AES-GCM encrypted, but independent sandboxed separate-process verification/custody still needs G03 hardening and final physical acceptance. OS worker/process tree cancellation for real rendering and full recovery matrices remain pending.
6. **30-second source film format still first delivery.** No long-form native streamer, high-quality final cinema audio, DCP-compliant output, calibrated 4K or sustained 3-hour render has been established. G09-D/E/F own finishing, long-running checkpoint custody, installation and distribution.

**Next engineering group:** **G09-D — Finishing and professional export**, including OCIO/ACES parity, native quality-checked sound mastering and standard-conforming picture/audio deliverables. **G09-E** handles sustained 30-minute then feature-length playback/render; **G09-F** distribution; G09-A/B/G03 remaining quality/security obligations remain independently open.
