# FILM03-07 — Expressive voice audition laboratory

**Goal:** NVP can audition alternate real voice performances for the exact canonical StoryBible ActorID/VoiceID and screenplay Take while preserving original audio and director creative choices.

A technical audition source binds ActorID, VoiceID, immutable take/script line, reference voice SHA and the director-authored expressive direction. External model identity/revision/run receipt hashes are simply technical provenance. The audio importer independently reopens PCM WAV bytes and measures duration, peaks/RMS, clipping and silence. Listening reviews cover word accuracy, naturalness, breaths, whisper, anger, softness, stress, emotion, retakes and voice continuity. **These measured properties do not themselves prove an excellent acting performance.**

Potential generators: Qwen3-TTS, IndexTTS, CosyVoice, Fish Audio Drama, OpenVoice V2 and Dia. These are optional *technical* research candidates, not bundled or approved voice engines. NVP does not manage actor contracts, consent paperwork, legal permissions, training rights or distribution approvals. Performers participate as creative collaborators: recording, acting, retakes and credit.

**Development status:** FILM03-07A source lab implemented; exact native acceptance and actual qualified expression engine remain OPEN. Earlier code paths that requested license/consent evidence fields have been removed from the active FILM03-09C code. No eSpeak NG cinematic voice fallback is restored. NVP must never confuse a model's generated file, its technical origin, a director's approved *source*, and a final artist-selected performance.
