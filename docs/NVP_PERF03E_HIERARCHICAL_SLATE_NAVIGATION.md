# PERF-03E — Hierarchical Digital Slate and filmmaker source navigation

## Delivered scope

Add immutable, creator-approved section grouping for original scene Take graphs and paged catalogs, and separately for committed Slate editorial cuts. Group children retain their exact object identity, revision, SHA-256, kind, count, and contiguous editorial offsets. No cross-catalog ancestry is fabricated. Existing leaf validation and bounds are retained. Each group has at most 64 children and at most three internal grouping levels; duplicate children, wrong family, cyclic/self references, wrong source digest and approval refusal are rejected. The host approves the exact canonical document digest, rechecks the composition after consent, and checks child revisions again inside the publishing transaction. Groups are navigation/organization, not approval to activate an edit or render.

FilmIndex adds an indexed per-project/kind keyset page with at most limit+1 query rows (page limit 64). Filtering can yield an empty page with a valid continuation. The camera source bridge scans at most 256 candidate scene rows per request, emits at most 64 camera-bearing scenes, and supports continuation without the former complete object-list allocation. Neither navigation route loads USD for scene summaries. Existing catalog pages and committed edit checks are reused; direct group child references are checked on reopen, descendants loaded on demand. Original catalog leaf bounds remain, and legacy leaf checks can still reopen a bounded leaf.

The read-only section directory and selected-section GET endpoints enforce project lock/access middleware, identity/revision/digest scope, bounded page size, and original source validation. Read P1-D/E v1 leaves and v2 manifests through the existing conform verifier. Exact timeline ticks remain strings in transport. Navigation is not source mutation, approval, job admission or output publication. Encrypted movies remain excluded until their native protected read bridge is integrated; never bypass the encrypted route guard.

Scene Library and Edit Film share an initially collapsed Browse scenes, takes and edits panel. Default 32 entries, lazy load, exact selection, next/previous/first-page controls, bounded cursor history, back navigation, abort/suppress stale results, retry and empty-page continuation. Camera picker gains first/next navigation across the former scan boundary. No engineering terms needed to operate these controls. New section publication currently exists at trusted host API/class level only: no new mutation HTTP route or filmmaker hierarchy creation/activation controls. Other legacy scene-list workflows are not globally migrated.
